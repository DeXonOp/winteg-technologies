GPS Tracking FAQ

What is GPS tracking software?

GPS tracking software collects location data from GPS devices and displays vehicles or assets on live maps. It powers fleet tracking, geofencing alerts, route history and driver analytics.

Can Winteg Technologies build a custom fleet tracking system?

Yes. Winteg Technologies builds custom fleet tracking systems with live maps (Mapbox GL), WebSocket location streaming, geofence alerts, route playback and fuel/driver analytics — scaled to millions of events per second.

What hardware works with your GPS tracking software?

Our GPS tracking software supports standard GPS trackers and OBD-II devices over TCP/UDP protocols, including Teltonika, Concox, Queclink and custom ESP32-based GPS hardware.
