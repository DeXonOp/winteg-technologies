ERP & CRM FAQ

What is the difference between ERP and CRM?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) manages internal operations — inventory, HR, finance and supply chain. CRM (Customer Relationship Management) manages customer interactions — sales pipeline, leads and support. Winteg Technologies builds both, often integrated.
