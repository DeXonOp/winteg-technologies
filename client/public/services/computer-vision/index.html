What computer vision services does Winteg Technologies provide?

Winteg Technologies provides facial recognition, object detection, defect detection, behavior analysis, smart surveillance and video analytics — deployed as edge AI on NVIDIA Jetson or in the cloud.

What is edge AI and why does it matter?

Edge AI runs vision models directly on the camera device instead of the cloud. This cuts latency to 12ms, works offline, saves bandwidth and keeps footage private — ideal for factory floors and security.
