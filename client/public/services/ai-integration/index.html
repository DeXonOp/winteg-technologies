AI Integration FAQ

What is AI integration?

AI integration connects AI models — chatbots, LLMs, machine learning or computer vision — into your existing software, workflows and databases so your business runs smarter without replacing your tools.

What AI integration services does Winteg Technologies provide?

Winteg Technologies provides custom AI chatbots, LLM fine-tuning, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, machine learning models, AI analytics dashboards and end-to-end process automation.

How long does AI integration take?

A chatbot or RAG assistant over your existing data typically ships in 3–6 weeks. Full-scale AI automation with custom models takes 2–4 months depending on data readiness and integration points.
