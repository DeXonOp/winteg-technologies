Telematics & IoT FAQ

What is telematics software development?

Telematics software development combines telecommunications and informatics to collect, transmit and visualize data from remote assets. Winteg Technologies builds telematics platforms that ingest MQTT data streams into time-series databases and display them on real-time dashboards.

Which industries use telematics and IoT monitoring?

Fleet and logistics, manufacturing, agriculture, energy, cold-chain logistics and construction all use telematics. Our IoT monitoring systems serve fleet operators, factories and equipment-rental companies worldwide.

How long does it take to build a telematics platform?

A telematics MVP with live dashboards and alerts typically ships in 6–10 weeks. Enterprise-scale IoT monitoring platforms with predictive maintenance take 3–5 months depending on device and data complexity.
